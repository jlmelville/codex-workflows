Use `$skill-retro` to produce a compact Skill Candidate Report for this
session. Keep the default output in chat; route it to the external inbox only
when I explicitly request `route` or `auto` mode.

qualifying workflow friction and disclose the number of records in the final
response. Papercut capture does not authorize candidate promotion or closure.

instructions now live in `skills/skill-retro/SKILL.md`.
