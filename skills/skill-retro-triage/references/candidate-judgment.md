# Candidate Judgment

Use for candidate intake, reconsideration, and fired drafts or ledgers. Apply
the parent skill's authority and shared evidence rules.

Choose `accept`, `defer`, `reject`, `split`, `merge`, or `no-change` for each
candidate. Evaluate concrete distinct evidence, durability and recurrence
likelihood, the exact guidance gap, expected benefit versus instruction and
maintenance cost, and the smallest natural destination. Keep the scope as
narrow as the evidence supports; broaden only with a credible transfer
mechanism. Separate the reusable kernel from repository-local wrappers.

Use `reject` or `no-change` when a candidate changes no decision an agent would
otherwise miss or costs more than the harm it prevents. Do not manufacture
verdict diversity or a rejection quota.

Before choosing a destination, state the changed decision, authority,
observation, or success condition. Prefer an existing principle. Keep shared
judgment in the default path, exact mechanics in routed references or scripts,
and distinct sanitized cases optional. Privacy, authority, evidence, and
completion contracts may remain on the default path even when unconditional.

Before a possible repeat or destination-repair verdict, run a bounded
`accepted-records` query. For same-decision source drift, use `no-change`, retain
the source repair, and update the original identity's destinations and commits
after publication. Preserve its behavioral verification. Recurrence of the same
decision and witness belongs in verification; if it arrived as a candidate,
reject or mark it `no-change` and direct the producer to the typed verification
route. Do not translate ambiguous intake into accepted-state evidence.

Public additions must pass payload review. Do not raise a baseline merely to
avoid judgment or require an offset from an unrelated skill. A separately
accepted capacity decision may own a justified addition's skill and aggregate
limits. Judge reductions independently for preserved decision value.

For `defer`, name the unjustified decision and missing evidence. Behavioral
uncertainty after a justified reversible implementation belongs in an
unverified accepted outcome. For `split` or `merge`, preserve all related opaque
candidate IDs and lineage.

Read the protocol's
[Triage And Archive](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#triage-and-archive)
before constructing a verdict for archival or a new or replacement deferral.
It owns `process`, resumable archival, `reconsider`, and the liveness contract:
predicate, observer, queue route, probe, next action, and close condition.
`review-queue` routes work but does not observe events; `destination-use` is
invalid until a supported matcher exists.

For fired drafts or ledgers, also read
[Drafts, Ledgers, And Cleanup](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#drafts-ledgers-and-cleanup).
A draft is a coherent new-skill kernel with activation criteria; a deferral
awaits evidence for a specific decision. Both need the same liveness contract
as open ledgers. Within authorized scope, execute fired non-skill actions and
activate, revise, or deprecate fired drafts instead of refreshing them
indefinitely. For public implementation, follow the parent route to
`report-to-patch.md`.
