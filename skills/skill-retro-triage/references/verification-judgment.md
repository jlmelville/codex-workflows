# Verification Judgment

Use for evidence about an accepted outcome, existing verification proposals or contradictions.
Apply the parent skill's authority and shared evidence rules. Read
[Verification Evidence](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#verification-evidence)
for eligible transitions, claim scopes and provenance limits.

Discover relevant outcomes with bounded `verification-context --destination TEXT --compact` and
expand selected accepted or proposal IDs with `verification-context --id ID`. For observed harm,
use `accepted-records` across all dispositions so inactive identities remain visible.
Judge the evidence once against the exact opportunity, basis and claim. A supplement must add
material evidence; its presence or a different claim label is insufficient. Preserve known guidance
context and judge independence.

For new evidence that warrants verification, follow
[Reviewed Verification Submission](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#reviewed-verification-submission).
Processing needs its own authority; preparation and capture permit no application or closure.
Link original observation IDs. Select optional closures only when their entire review is complete;
a source link alone requests no closure. Leave observations open when another claim, source change
or action still needs review. Closure does not resolve an accepted contradiction or authorize
another owner's work. Legacy candidates and papercuts retain their existing closure operations.

When evidence does not warrant verification, record that conclusion on the observation. Defer only
a named unresolved decision. For existing legacy proposals, follow
[Legacy Verification Processing](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#legacy-verification-processing);
new legacy creation remains retired.

Keep the prepared VP identity and completed input. After interruption or a conflicting intervening
review, follow [Verification Recovery](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#verification-recovery).
Reuse the original submission; resolution requires explicit authorized judgment and preserves
original evidence and any applied verification.

For contradictions, follow [Contradiction Resolution](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#contradiction-resolution).
Judge whether source correction, removal or justified retention is warranted; preserve contradiction
and valid positive evidence. A new identity requires a newly accepted corrected decision
through [shared-change review](candidate-judgment.md). Agreed work that remains needs an authorized
residual ledger under [Drafts, Ledgers, And Cleanup](../../skill-retro/references/state-protocol.md#drafts-ledgers-and-cleanup).
When only verification processing is authorized, report source or residual-action proposals that
still need authority. Finish authorized state processing with helper `validate`.
