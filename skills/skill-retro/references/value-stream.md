# Value-Stream Judgment

Use the user's objective and the values in applicable guidance to examine how
work moves from request to useful result. Notice concerns at consequential
choices while working, then revisit the journey at a retrospective checkpoint.
Papercut capture and skill retrospectives are specialized ways to preserve
what this judgment discovers; their signal lists do not bound what can be
noticed.

Ask where effort, evidence, or decisions stopped serving the user. Follow a
concrete concern back to the choice that produced it and forward to its
consequence. A clean final artifact can coexist with avoidable detours, repeated
computation, unnecessary machinery, or demands on the owner's attention.
Successful work can also reveal a simpler procedure worth reusing.

The following questions illustrate values already present in this corpus.
Choose those relevant to the work and pursue other concerns the evidence
reveals; this is an open inquiry, not a checklist to complete:

- **Useful progress:** Which steps advanced the user's task, and which became
  work only because the chosen process demanded them? Grounded in
  [planning](../../planning-workflow/SKILL.md) and
  [semantic authoring](../../codex-skill-repo/references/semantic-authoring.md).
- **Evidence at the claim's boundary:** What did the observation establish,
  and where did inference become a stronger reported claim? Which material
  premises could change the recommendation, and are they checked or explicitly
  uncertain? Grounded in
  [retrospective admission](../SKILL.md) and
  [acceptance evidence](../../planning-workflow/references/acceptance-evidence.md).
- **Proportionate effort:** What repeated work, abstraction, scan, or review
  imposed cost without a corresponding consumer benefit? Which accepted
  requirement or concrete uncertainty justifies added records, approvals, reviews,
  or verification? Grounded in
  [architecture review](../../architecture-audit/SKILL.md) and
  [candidate judgment](../../skill-retro-triage/references/candidate-judgment.md).
- **Owner attention and control:** Where did an agent unnecessarily return a
  settled decision to the user, or infer authority the user had not given?
  Grounded in [triage authority](../../skill-retro-triage/SKILL.md) and
  [planning acceptance](../../planning-workflow/SKILL.md).

Applicable instructions govern the action; this reference helps discover a
question and grants no additional authority. Improve the current work within
scope when justified. Preserve qualifying fresh friction through
`$papercut-capture`; use `$skill-retro` for an admitted reusable missing delta
or evidence matching an accepted opportunity. Already-covered guidance calls
for diagnosing discovery, interpretation, or effectiveness before adding prose.
State observed behavior and consequence separately from suspected causes.
An inconclusive concern or a justified no-change decision needs no record.
